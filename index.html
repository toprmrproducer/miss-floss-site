What exactly is Miss Floss?

Miss Floss is a HIPAA-compliant AI dental receptionist. She answers your phone, books appointments directly into your PMS, verifies insurance, handles recall and treatment-plan follow-up, and texts patients after the call — 24/7, in 40+ languages.

Will my patients know they're talking to AI?

Most don't. Miss Floss is disclosed as an AI assistant when asked, but the voice is warm, the pacing is natural, and she handles interruptions like a human. Our practices regularly hear "your front desk is so helpful" from patients who never realized.

Is Miss Floss HIPAA compliant?

Yes. SOC 2 Type II audited, HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, BAA included on every plan. PHI is encrypted at rest and in transit, with access logs on every record. PCI compliance for phone payments.

Which PMS does she integrate with?

Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Curve, Carestack, Denticon, Practice-Web, and most others via our universal API. Native two-way sync — Miss Floss writes appointments straight to your schedule, no copy-paste, no double bookings.

Can she verify insurance in real time?

Yes. Miss Floss pulls eligibility, plan details, deductibles, and remaining benefits from 700+ carriers — before the patient hangs up. No more eligibility chases at 8am.

How long does onboarding take?

48 hours from contract to first answered call. We handle PMS integration, voice training, call-flow setup, and number forwarding. You approve the script. She goes live.

What if she can't handle a call?

She warm-transfers to your team or takes a callback with full context. Every escalation gets logged and added to her training. Within two weeks, her handoff rate is under 5%.

What does Miss Floss cost?

Less than a part-time front-desk hire. Pricing scales with call volume, not seats. Solo practices typically pay under $1k/mo; DSOs get volume pricing. Book a demo for an exact quote on your call volume.
