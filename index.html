Which practice management systems do you support?

Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and Curve out of the box. If you run something else, ask us. We are likely already integrating with it.

Is MissFloss.ai HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and we sign a BAA with every practice before go-live.

How long does onboarding take?

Most practices are live within five business days. We handle the PMS connection, voice training, and message review with your team.

Will it replace my front desk?

No. It hands your front desk the time they were losing to confirmations and recall calls, so they can focus on patients in front of them.

How is pricing structured?

Flat monthly pricing per location, with no per-message fees. We share exact numbers on the demo call once we know your practice size.
